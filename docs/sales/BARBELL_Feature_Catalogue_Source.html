Getting started · Home

A personal app from the first minute

Members join the gym's Barbell in seconds. From then on, Home tells them what matters today, and nothing they don't need.

Join with the gym's code

New members sign up with the gym's sign-up code and are placed in the right gym automatically. No manual matching by staff.

Onboarding that builds real targets

Six quick questions about the member's body, goal and experience set personal calorie and macro targets instead of a generic average, and progress is measured from their own starting point. Everything can be changed later.

Guided tour

A short walkthrough of the main screens on first use, replayable any time from Help.

Today at a glance

Today's session with its muscle focus and length, calories left with protein, carbs and fat, today's burn, progress toward the goal weight, how busy the gym is right now, and what the gym community has been doing.

A Home that's theirs

Members choose which cards appear and in what order. Cards appear only when they have something to show.

Coach Brief

A daily list of priorities worked out from the member's own logs: over target on calories, short on water, protein reached, today's session done. Each comes with a priority level and a link to act on it. Built on rules, not guesswork, and it needs no AI key.

Reminders that fit the day

The notification bell carries reminders for today's workout, tomorrow's session, an unfinished workout, rest days, meal logging, water at the member's chosen interval, and a daily recap.

In control of every nudge

Each reminder type can be switched on or off, with a water interval, a recap time and quiet hours. If the member allows it, reminders also appear as device notifications while Barbell is open in a background tab.

Training

Every session planned, logged and progressed

The trainer's program decides what's on today. Barbell remembers every set, so each session builds on the last.

Today's Training

The member's weekly program serves the right session each day automatically: name, muscle focus, exercises with sets, reps and weight, estimated duration and a this-week strip showing what's done.

Progressive overload, built in

Each lift shows what was done last time and what to try today, for example "Last time 82.5 kg × 8 · today try 85 kg × 8". The session header says how many lifts are ready to progress.

Live workout session

A running timer and a set-by-set log of reps and weight, with one tap to reuse last time's numbers. Members can add or remove sets and add exercises. Progress saves as they go, so a session can be resumed.

Effort and calories, honestly

After a session the member rates the effort. Barbell stores the calories burned as an estimate with a range (for example 221 kcal, 156–286), never as a false precise figure.

Personal records, automatically

Completing a workout detects new records for heaviest weight, estimated one-rep max and best set volume, and shows them in the session recap.

Build today

Where the gym allows it, members build their own session from the exercise library, filtered by muscle group and equipment. It replaces today's session.

My workouts planner

Saved sessions and a personal weekly schedule, with Do today, Edit, Copy and Delete on each.

Log a past workout

Forgot to start the app? Members record the session afterwards with date, start and end time, exercises and effort. Barbell warns if it overlaps another session.

Cardio and sports

40+ activities across gym machines (treadmill, rower, air bike, ski erg), outdoor training and sports such as cricket, badminton, football, kabaddi, yoga and boxing. Run live or log afterwards.

Training history

Every completed and missed session, plus a day view that shows exactly what was trained and eaten on any date.

287-exercise library

Every exercise tagged with primary and secondary muscles, equipment and difficulty, with an animated exercise visual.

Share a workout

Send any session as a link. Anyone can preview it; a member who signs in imports it to today or to their planner.

My PRs and strength trends

Recent records and a "where your strength is moving" list showing each lift's change over the weeks, for example lateral raise +93.8% in 8 weeks.

Nutrition

Food logging that understands Indian meals

Most food apps struggle with "2 roti, dal and chai". Barbell's food engine knows katoris, rotis and home cooking, and calculates every value from published food-composition data.

Today's Fuel

One dial for calories left (or over), with protein, carbs and fat, the day's planned meals and everything logged.

Type a meal in plain words

Members write what they ate the way they'd say it. Barbell splits it into foods, picks a sensible portion (a roti, a katori of dal, a cup of chai) and lets them adjust quantities before logging.

Or say it

A microphone button turns speech into the same meal entry, tuned for Indian English food words. It uses the browser's built-in speech recognition (Chrome, Edge, Safari) and no third-party service.

21,000+ foods, real portions

Search across the Indian Food Composition Tables, 1,014 Indian dishes, USDA and Canadian data and Open Food Facts packaged products. Pick portions in bowls, katoris, pieces or grams, and set the cooking oil from low to high.

Barcode scanning

Scan a packaged product with the camera to get its brand, serving and full nutrition. Lookups check Barbell's own cache and bundled data before the live Open Food Facts database.

Usuals and repeat

Barbell learns what a member usually eats at each meal and keeps recent meals one tap away. Any past meal can be repeated today.

Custom foods and saved meals

Add family recipes with their own macros and units, save regular meals, and log either in one step.

Share meals

Send saved meals and foods as a link; the recipient saves them to their own diet.

Targets that fit the person

Daily calories and macros recommended from metabolic rate, activity and goal, or set by hand.

Calorie rebalancing

After an over-target day, Barbell offers to spread the surplus across the coming days: Easy, Moderate, Aggressive, Intense or Custom. For example, 1,270 kcal over becomes −320 kcal a day for four days, with protein protected.

Water and supplements

Quick +250 ml / +500 ml water logging against a daily target, and a supplement list with dose and time.

Net energy

Intake against total burn for the day, with a running energy-balance history.

Nutrition Tracker

Browse any date, compare target and actual, and view 7-day, 30-day, 3-month, 6-month or custom ranges with averages. The day can start at 3–6 am so a late dinner counts to the night it was eaten.

Numbers members can rely on. Portions and nutrition values come from Barbell's food engine, which matches each food against published food-composition data (the Indian Food Composition Tables, the Indian dish database, USDA and Open Food Facts) and weighs portions by each food's own serving size. Where a portion is converted or approximate, it is labelled as an estimate rather than shown as exact.
Progress & body

Progress members can see and trust

Clear trends, honest estimates and milestones that reward consistency, not just results.

Progress dashboard

A plain-language headline ("Trending down 0.4 kg/week"), weight change, sessions, adherence and records over 7 days to 1 year.

Explore your data

Body, Training, Nutrition and Energy views, each comparable with the previous period. Touch any chart to read a day.

Goal ETA

"About 13 weeks from your target", projected from the current trend and labelled as an estimate, not a promise.

Weight and measurements

Weight feeds straight into the trend and goal. Waist, chest, arms, thighs, hips and neck can be back-dated, corrected or deleted, and readings far outside the normal range are flagged before saving.

Custom metrics

Resting heart rate, steps, a lift to watch: members track anything else with its own unit and target.

Where the work went

Total volume and sets per muscle group, so members see what they're actually training.

120 milestones

Five categories (Training, Strength, Consistency, Nutrition and Body), each with progressive levels and a "closest to earning" shortlist.

Progress photos

Front, side and back photos taken by the coach appear on Progress as a before/after comparison, with the days between them.

Today's burn, explained

Resting energy, workouts and cardio, each labelled with its source and whether it was measured or estimated.

Daily health intelligence

Logged workouts and the member's profile combine into daily energy and training-load figures. It works with no wearable and never double-counts energy across sources.

Community & competition

A gym community that keeps people coming back

Optional and private by default. Members share only what they choose, and weight, food, sleep and health data are never shared.

Gym community

Members opt in and see who was active today, workouts and records this week, their streak, weekly consistency and a community activity calendar.

Leaderboards

Today, this week or this month, ranked by workouts or streaks. The owner can turn leaderboards off.

Activity feed

Share a finished workout, react with fire, strong, clap or like, comment, and copy anyone's shared workout into your own planner. Show everyone, or only people you follow.

Members and following

A members directory with follow, which shapes the feed and who can see a member's records.

Private friend communities

Train with friends at other gyms, or no gym at all. Create an invite-only community with its own colour and mark, invite by code or link, and compete on leaderboards and challenges for workouts, volume or records.

Privacy controls

Members choose who sees their personal records (everyone at the gym, only followers, or nobody) and what their feed shows.

Tournaments for every member

Enter a gym's tournament from the app, by code or by scanning its QR. Anyone with the link can follow the public tournament page.

Host one yourself

Any member can host: name it, pick events, set the venue and prize, invite people. Hosts record scores live and rankings update instantly.

Podiums and points

Results show each event's ranking, an overall podium (10, 7, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 points by place), gold, silver and bronze, and gym-versus-gym standings.

Trophy cabinet

Every member has a username and a cabinet of tournaments played, medals and titles. They can choose to appear in search so hosts at other gyms can invite them.

Discover

Live, upcoming and finished tournaments from the member's gym, plus open ones from other gyms.

Profile, privacy & data

Personal, private and under the member's control

Profile and goals

A journey card (start, now, target, percentage of the way) and goal setup: goal type, target weight and date, experience and body details.

My equipment

Nine equipment types, from barbell and cables to bands and bodyweight, so plans use what's actually available.

Coach preferences

When they train, how long a session can be, what kit they have, foods they actually eat, exercises to avoid, and anything else worth knowing, such as injuries or travel.

Message your coach

A direct thread with the trainer from the Profile page; replies arrive in the notification bell.

Account and display

Name, phone and profile photo; a password change that requires the current password; system, light or dark appearance; metric or imperial units, which change only the display, never the data.

Cookies and terms

Essential cookies only by default, with preferences, analytics and marketing off until the member turns them on. Terms are accepted at first sign-in.

Download or delete

One tap downloads everything (profile, workouts, food, measurements, payments, devices) as a single file. Members can permanently delete their account by typing their email to confirm.

Today's planned meals and what was actually logged, plan targets, a 30-day history, and Assign plan.

Photos

Upload front, side and back photos. A before/after slider compares them, and the client sees them on their Progress page.

Membership

Plan and real status (for example lapsed and how long ago), plus attendance over the last 90 days and the last visit.

Client analysis

One click analyses the client's recorded data and proposes an insight with a recommended action, such as the next progression target. The trainer accepts, modifies or dismisses it. Every insight comes only from measured, calculated or estimated data.

Messages

The conversation with the client, right inside their profile.

Programming

Build once, assign in seconds

Workout builder

Templates filtered by type and sorted by recent, A–Z or longest. Add exercises with sets, reps, weight and rest; reorder them; preview with a 3D muscle body; duplicate a template; assign it to a client on any date.

Custom exercises

Add exercises the library doesn't have; they become part of the gym's own library.

Weekly programs

Push/Pull/Legs (3 to 6 days), Upper/Lower, Full Body (2 or 3 days) or custom. Map each day to a template and the client's Workout page serves the right session every day, automatically.

Nutrition builder

Plans with calorie and macro targets and meals for each slot, from breakfast to before bed, each with time, calories and macros, using realistic Indian meals. Assign to any client.

Stay ahead

Catch the drop-off before it happens

Risk alerts

Signals detected from real client data: weight plateau, protein below 70% of target, no workout for several days, low workout adherence, missed weekly check-in, low nutrition adherence and inactivity. Each has a severity and an explanation with the numbers behind it, and can be marked read, followed up or dismissed. Alerts are shared across the gym's coaching team, and each one names whose client it is.

Weekly reports

A week in review generated from tracked data: weight change, workouts completed, water, an adherence breakdown, a coach summary, a next-week action and a 7-day log. Send to client delivers it to their notification bell and message thread.

Messages

A direct line to each client, with quick templates for workout updates, nutrition updates and check-in reminders, and unread counts per client.

Tick planned meals

Mark a client's planned meals as eaten from their Nutrition view, for example after a check-in conversation.

Your hours & events

Your own record, kept straight

My attendance

Scan the gym's Arriving and Leaving codes; see this week's, 30-day or 90-day days present, total hours, average day and late count. Missed a scan? Request a correction and the owner approves it.

Tournaments

Trainers host and score tournaments just like owners, and can be added as co-hosts to run events live.
